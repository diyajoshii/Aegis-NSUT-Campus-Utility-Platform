📱 Aegis-NSUT: Your Smart Campus Companion
Welcome to Aegis, the all-in-one mobile solution crafted for students of NSUT to simplify academic and campus life. Aegis brings together tools for scholarship assistance, lost & found tracking, skill-based testing, and syllabus support — all powered by smart automation and AI.

🚀 Overview
College life can be overwhelming — whether it’s keeping up with deadlines, improving skills, finding misplaced belongings, or just navigating through your syllabus. Aegis aims to solve all that, in one integrated platform.
🌟 Core Modules
🎓 ScholarAid – Find the Right Scholarship, Effortlessly
A personalized scholarship recommendation system powered by AI chatbot Pathya.

🤖 Pathya: AI chatbot that helps students discover relevant scholarships
🔍 Search & filter by state, category, or eligibility
🗂️ Explore central and state-level schemes
📥 Download details / 📤 Apply directly via in-app links
📅 Get deadline reminders
🔍 Reclaimr – Real-Time Lost & Found System
Helping students find lost items on campus with intelligent item-matching.

🔎 Post lost/found items with descriptions and images
🧠 AI-based smart matching engine
✅ Verified returns via student ID confirmation
🔔 Instant notifications for matched items
💻 SkillX – Proctored Skill Assessments
A secure skill-testing platform for clubs, societies, and individuals.

🧪 MCQs + Coding rounds across 15+ domains (C++, Java, ML, Web Dev, etc.)
🧑‍💻 Clubs can create & host their own tests
📸 Proctored tests using tiny_face_detector_model
Detects tab switches, multiple faces, and screen recording attempts
Full-screen mode with auto-submission on repeated violations
📊 Instant results with:
Test scores
Violation summary
Average, highest, and latest score analytics
📚 Sylly – Your Academic Buddy
An AI-powered syllabus guide and study planner to stay organized.

🤖 Ask academic doubts and get syllabus-aligned answers
📅 Schedule study reminders
📓 Auto-generate notes based on syllabus topics
📘 Download or share customized study plans
